My Innovative Ideas

CyberSathi – Digital Helper for Small Businesses

CyberSathi is both an idea and an ongoing project aimed at helping small shopkeepers and local businesses go digital. The platform will allow shop owners to maintain product lists, generate digital bills, manage customers, and track daily sales easily. It is especially targeted for rural and semi-urban areas where businesses still operate manually. The vision is to create a simple, affordable and easy-to-use system for non-technical users.

LocaLide – Rural Ride Sharing Platform

LocaLide is a startup idea similar to Ola and Rapido but focused on rural and small-town areas. The idea is that anyone traveling on a route can offer rides to nearby people going in the same direction. Users can request rides, share location, negotiate fares and travel together. This idea aims to solve transportation problems in villages where public transport is limited.

Smart Irrigation Car

This is an agriculture-based innovation idea. A small automated car equipped with sensors will move through fields, check soil moisture levels and automatically water crops where needed. It will be controlled through WiFi and IoT technology. The aim is to save water, time and manual labor for farmers.
